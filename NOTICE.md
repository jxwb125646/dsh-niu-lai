# Audio notice

The two default sound clips shipped in `assets/` and embedded in the browser
bundle (`src/client/assets.ts`, `lib/client.js`) are extracted from the
Chinese movie **《牛来》**:

- `assets/妈妈.mp3` — the viral cow "妈妈" sound
- `assets/牛来.mp3` — the "牛来" phrase

These clips are the copyrighted audio of the movie's producers and are
included here **only for personal, non-commercial fan use**. They are NOT
covered by the repository's MIT license, which applies solely to the source
code in this repository.

If you are the rights holder and object to this inclusion, please open an
issue and the clips will be removed promptly.

Users who want fully clear rights can replace both clips with their own
recordings from the plugin's Settings tab (设置 → 插件 → 牛来音效).
